GLI Global 2022 numerical reference data

Bowerman C, Bhakta NR, Brazzale D, et al. A race-neutral approach to the
interpretation of lung function measurements. AJRCCM 2023;207:768-774.
https://doi.org/10.1164/rccm.202205-0963OC
Published workbook: gli_global_lookuptables_dec6.xlsx.
GLI equations/tables are available for software implementation:
https://gli-calculator.ersnet.org/

Spline CSV transcription and independent numerical validation grid obtained
from pft 1.0.4, commit 7489d487bb5bffd5418e66362ebeb0a7332c9916:
https://github.com/overdodactyl/pft
pft's build_gli_2022.R regenerates these CSVs from the published GLI workbook.
Its oracle grid was calculated independently with rspiro (30 demographic cases).
Our runtime does not execute or require R, pft, rspiro, or a remote service.
Original CSV SHA256 hashes: docs/gli-global-sources.json in the source project.
